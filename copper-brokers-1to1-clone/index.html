What We Do

Simple, Reliable HVAC Equipment Removal

At Copper Brokers LLC, we specialize in the recycling, recovery, and transportation of HVAC equipment and non-ferrous metals. We work with contractors, businesses, and property managers to provide efficient scrap metal solutions while paying competitive prices for recyclable materials. Our services include purchasing copper, aluminum, brass, stainless steel, ACR, and other non-ferrous metals, recycling HVAC units and components, providing transportation for new RTU installations, coordinating rooftop unit removals, and supplying dumpsters for large-scale commercial projects. We handle the heavy lifting, loading, and logistics so our customers can focus on their business while maximizing the value of their scrap materials.

As a veteran-owned company, we take pride in delivering dependable service, fair pricing, and professional solutions for every job.
