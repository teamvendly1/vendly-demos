About Us

At Copper Brokers LLC, we are dedicated to making recycling straightforward, transparent, and rewarding for everyone involved. As a proud veteran-owned and operated company, we bring years of experience to the table, working closely with HVAC contractors, businesses, and property owners. Our mission is to provide reliable recycling and scrap metal solutions that not only benefit our clients but also contribute positively to our community and environment. Trust us to handle your recycling needs with integrity and expertise.

Meet the Owner

My name is Cory Bowers, owner and operator of this business and a proud United States Navy veteran. My time in the Navy instilled values that continue to guide me everyday discipline, accountability, integrity, and a strong commitment to serving others. After completing my military service, I knew I wanted to continue helping people, just in a different way. That desire ultimately led me to build this business from the ground up.

I founded this company with a simple mission: to provide honest, dependable HVAC recycling and equipment breakdown services while helping businesses and contractors handle their material disposal the right way.

Whether it's dismantling units, recovering valuable materials like copper and wiring, or keeping a job site clean and efficient, I understand that this work is more than just getting rid of equipment, it's about doing the job properly and making the process easier for the customer.

In many cases, the materials we recover like copper and metal have real value. That means customers may even be compensated for the scrap from their HVAC units, turning what would normally be waste into something beneficial.

I take pride in approaching every project with professionalism, respect, and attention to detail. My goal is to make sure every unit is handled correctly, materials are separated efficiently, and the job is done right from start to finish.

As a business owner, I believe in doing things the right way. When I commit to a job, I show up on time, communicate clearly, and work hard to exceed expectations. Every customer is treated with the same level of respect and care because building trust is just as important as completing the job itself. Unlike large corporations, when you work with us, you're working directly with the owner. I take personal pride in the quality of our work, the relationships we build, and the reputation we earn within our community. This business is more than a source of income it's a reflection of the values I learned through military service and the commitment I have to serving others. I am grateful for every opportunity to serve our customers and our community, and I look forward to earning your trust for years to come.

Cory Bowers
