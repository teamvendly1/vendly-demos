# Design references — Scratch Kitchen & Taproom demo

Note on method: WebSearch was unavailable in this session (permission denied), so
these are not freshly browsed. They are restaurants/brands I am highly confident
are real, well-known, established businesses from prior general knowledge, named
here only as stylistic reference points (layout/tone), not copied from directly.

1. **Husk Restaurant** (Charleston, SC) — huskrestaurant.com. Chef Sean Brock's
   James Beard-recognized Southern restaurant. Reference for: warm, ingredient-
   forward Southern-food storytelling and a photography-led home page.
2. **Cracker Barrel Old Country Store** — crackerbarrel.com. National Southern-
   style restaurant chain. Reference for: broad, welcoming Southern-hospitality
   tone in copy, simple wayfinding to location/hours/menu.
3. **Yard House** — yardhouse.com. American gastropub chain built around a large
   draft-beer program. Reference for: pairing a "kitchen" identity with a "tap"
   identity in one brand, which is exactly Scratch Kitchen & Taproom's own name.
4. **World of Beer** — worldofbeer.com. Beer-focused taproom/bar concept.
   Reference for: taproom-specific visual language (dark moody photography,
   glassware close-ups) worth borrowing for the hero treatment.
5. **Poogan's Porch** — poogansporch.com. Historic Charleston Southern-cuisine
   restaurant in a converted house. Reference for: a factbox/contact block that
   feels like a warm neighborhood spot rather than a chain.

These informed tone and layout thinking only. No copy, code, or imagery was
copied from any of the above; every fact and photo used in index.html traces
back to FILL.json / HARVEST.json / ASSET-LEDGER.json for this business.
