Stephen Lane3 years ago
There is one word that describes the work done by Marcos, Randi and company (M&R Painting), EXCELLENT. They do prep the way it is supposed to be done, pay attention to the details, take pride in their work … and it shows. I originally hired M&R Painting to restore and paint my cedar siding salt box style colonial home. Since the previous painters improperly applied latex over oil-based paint, the paint on the entire house was peeling. Marcos and Randi started by stripping off all the old peeling paint using infrared heaters. Once this was completed (which took almost 3 weeks) they lightly sanded all the siding, applied one coat of oil-based primer, sanded again, then applied two-coats of latex-based paint. The results were fantastic, the house looks like new! In addition, Diego, who also doubles as a skilled carpenter, replaced a lot of the rotten trim boards and window stills, and did an outstanding job. Recently, I hired M&R back to paint three rooms in my house (living room, dining room and bedroom), plus the kitchen ceiling. They made sure to put down resin paper (with tape) and drop clothes to protect the floors, covered all the furniture with plastic, and walled off the rooms with plastic sheeting to keep the sanding dust from getting into other parts of the house. As with the exterior work, the crew arrived promptly at 8am and finished painting at 4pm, then proceeded to make sure everything was cleaned up and properly put away before they left the job at 4:30pm. Although painting interior spaces can be disruptive, Marcos and Randi did everything they could to minimize the intrusion. As a testament to their skill as painters, I was always surprised to see that they often wore black shoes and black pants to the painting jobs in the morning ... and left at the end of the day with not a drop of paint spilled on anything! M&R Painting gets my highest recommendation (5 out of 5 stars). They do high quality work at reasonable prices, are clean and neat, and are a pleasure to do business with. If you are especially interested in restoration work (historical or otherwise), these are definitely the guys for you.
