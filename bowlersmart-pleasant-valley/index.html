What the shop does

Not a generic sports aisle - a bowling pro shop with services built around how you actually throw.

Ball Fitting

Span, pitch, and weight matched to your hand and style so the ball comes off clean instead of fighting you.

Expert Drilling

Custom layouts drilled in-house for house shots and sport patterns - bring your new ball or pick one from the wall.

Bowling Balls

A deep bench of performance and entry balls so league bowlers and newbies are not stuck with one shelf.

Bags

Single, double, and roller bags that survive weekly league nights without dumping your shoes in the parking lot.

Shoes

Rental replacements and performance shoes sized correctly - ask the shop what they have in stock today.

Accessories

Tape, grips, towels, and the small gear that keeps a series from falling apart mid-block.

Get fitted before you buy blind.

  • Talk through your average and oil conditions
  • Measure for a layout that fits your release
  • Drill on site inside the Pleasant Valley center
  • Walk back to the lanes the same visit when timing allows

Call ahead if you need a specific brand or weight - the shop can tell you what is on the wall today.
