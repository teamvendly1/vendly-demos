Confirmed news

What has actually happened, not what people are guessing.

Every item below is something that was officially announced or actually aired, with a real source linked at the bottom of the page. No leaks treated as confirmed, no fan art passed off as official.

  • Release date

    Launch locked for November 19, 2026

    The publisher reconfirmed the date on its most recent earnings call, with no further delay announced. The game ships on PS5, PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X|S. No PC release date has been announced yet.

    Status as of August 2026

  • Pricing

    Standard and Ultimate editions priced at $79.99 and $99.99

    Official US pricing is confirmed for both editions. Pre-orders opened worldwide earlier this summer. Pricing outside the US varies by region and retailer.

    Status as of August 2026

    Roughly 26 minutes of real, in-game gameplay aired on Netflix

    The extended look was captured entirely from in-game footage running on PS5 and followed the two playable leads through the game's fictional setting. It streamed on Netflix first, then went up publicly shortly after on the publisher's own channels.

    Aired August 27, 2026
