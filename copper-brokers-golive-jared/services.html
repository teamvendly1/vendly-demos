What We Offer

At Copper Brokers LLC, we provide reliable scrap metal recycling and transportation solutions tailored to the HVAC, commercial, and industrial industries. We're committed to competitive pricing, dependable service, and hassle-free metal recovery.

Reliable HVAC recycling and equipment breakdown services you can count on.

HVAC Recycling: Safely dismantling HVAC units and separating recyclable materials such as metals, coils, and components. Our process ensures responsible disposal and helps reduce environmental waste.

Equipment Breakdown: Our team breaks down HVAC equipment after pickup, removing usable materials and disposing of non-recyclable parts properly. This frees up space and keeps your job site clean, while paying top dollar for your equipment.

Pickup Services: We provide convenient pickup services for HVAC units and equipment. Whether you have a single unit or a large quantity, we'll schedule a pickup time that works for you and handle the removal.

Dumpster Services: Roll-off dumpsters and collection containers for contractors, property managers, and large corporations, with same-day pickup and delivery available.

Transportation Services: We provide reliable transportation and delivery services, ensuring your equipment arrives safely and on schedule at your job site or designated location.

Non-Ferrous Metal Purchasing: We purchase copper, aluminum, brass, stainless steel, insulated wire, ACR, and other non-ferrous metals at competitive market prices to help you maximize the value of your scrap metal.
