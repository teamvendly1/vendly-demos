The Problem

Every Old Unit Sitting There Is Money You're Not Collecting

Contractors and property managers end up storing old HVAC equipment because pulling it, breaking it down, and hauling it off takes time nobody has. Meanwhile the copper, aluminum, and brass inside it is worth real money at the yard, money that's just sitting on a rooftop or in a warehouse corner instead of in your pocket.

What We Do

We Do The Heavy Lifting. You Get Paid For The Scrap.

  • You get compensated for the copper, aluminum, brass, and other non-ferrous metal coming out of your units, not stuck with a landfill fee.
  • We handle the breakdown, loading, and hauling, so your crew stays on the job instead of babysitting scrap.
  • One call schedules the pickup, whether it's a single unit or a whole rooftop's worth.

At Copper Brokers LLC, we specialize in the recycling, recovery, and transportation of HVAC equipment and non-ferrous metals: purchasing copper, aluminum, brass, stainless steel, ACR, and other non-ferrous metals, recycling HVAC units and components, transporting new RTU installs, coordinating rooftop unit removals, and supplying dumpsters for large-scale commercial projects. As a veteran-owned company, we take pride in delivering dependable service, fair pricing, and professional solutions for every job.
