Why "real" billable hours matters: a 40-hour week almost never means 40 billed hours. Driving between jobs, writing quotes, ordering parts, and admin all eat real time that a customer never sees on an invoice. Guessing your rate off a 40-hour week is the single most common way trades businesses under-price themselves.
