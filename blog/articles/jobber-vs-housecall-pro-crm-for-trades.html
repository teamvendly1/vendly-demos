Jobber vs Housecall Pro: Which Field Service CRM Is Actually Worth It

A plan-by-plan look at what Jobber and Housecall Pro actually cost and include, based on their own pricing pages, for a trade business trying to pick one.

Most trade businesses outgrow a paper clipboard and a shared calendar app around the same time: once there is more than one truck, or more than a handful of jobs a week to keep straight. Jobber and Housecall Pro are the two names that come up most often when someone starts looking for a real field service CRM. Both do scheduling, quotes, invoicing, and customer records in one place. The real differences are in what each plan actually includes and how the price climbs as a crew grows, so this is a straight comparison of what each company itself publishes, checked directly against their pricing pages.

Jobber's plans

Jobber has four tiers, all priced as an annual-prepay monthly rate: Core at $21/month for one user with online booking, quotes, invoicing with online payments, a basic website, and reporting. Connect at $70/month, which includes five users and adds automated reminders, automatic payment collection, invoice follow-ups, and QuickBooks sync. Grow at $105/month, which includes ten users and adds customizable quotes with photos, job costing, two-way texting, and automations. Plus at $280/month, which includes fifteen users and adds centralized job files, a sales pipeline view, a marketing suite, and an AI receptionist feature. Extra users beyond what is included run $29/month each on every tier. Jobber offers a 14-day free trial with full access to the Grow-tier feature set and does not require a credit card to start.

Housecall Pro's plans

Housecall Pro also has three main tiers. Basic runs $59/month billed annually (or $79/month billed monthly) for one user, and covers online booking, invoicing and payments, scheduling and dispatching, quotes and proposals, and review requests. Essentials runs $149/month annually ($189 monthly) for five users, adding route optimization, flat-rate pricing, photo reports, GPS tracking of employees, and QuickBooks Online sync. Extra users on Essentials run $100/month each. MAX runs $299/month annually ($329 monthly) for eight users, adding a full sales proposal tool, recurring service plans, open API access, and a dedicated onboarding specialist, with extra users at $75/month each. Housecall Pro's free trial is also 14 days, giving full access to the top MAX-tier feature set with no credit card required.

Where the price actually lands for a small crew

For a true one-person operation, Jobber Core at $21/month is noticeably cheaper than anything Housecall Pro offers, since Housecall Pro's cheapest tier starts around $59 to $79 a month. Once you need five users, the comparison flips: Jobber Connect at $70/month for five users undercuts Housecall Pro Essentials at $149 to $189/month for the same headcount by a wide margin. Housecall Pro's dispatching and route optimization tools are built for teams running multiple trucks at once, which is part of why its middle and top tiers cost more. If routing and driver tracking genuinely matter to how the business runs day to day, that cost may be worth it. If the business is mostly one or two people who each run their own day, it usually is not.

Feature differences worth knowing about

  • Jobber's website builder is included even on its cheapest plan, which Housecall Pro does not offer as a comparable built-in feature
  • Housecall Pro's route optimization and GPS employee tracking are more built-out than Jobber's equivalent tools, and matter more the more trucks are on the road at once
  • Both sync with QuickBooks Online, but only on their mid and upper tiers, not their cheapest plan
  • Both offer a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, so trying both on a real week of scheduling before committing costs nothing but time

The honest bottom line

Neither one is simply better. A solo operator or a two-person crew usually gets more for less money out of Jobber. A business running several trucks that genuinely needs route optimization and driver tracking day to day often gets more real value out of Housecall Pro, even at the higher price. Both numbers above are exactly what each company lists on its own pricing page as of when this was written, and SaaS pricing changes without much notice, so check the current numbers on their sites before deciding.
