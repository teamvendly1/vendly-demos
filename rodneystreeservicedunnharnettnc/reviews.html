We contacted Rodney's tree service based off of reviews. Overall, the job of cutting trees down was done well, and I would recommend this company. There's just a few things to be cautious of with the service because, as many do, the contract states they do not take responsibility for any damage done on the lawn. (HOWEVER, in my case they did come back to fix the issues from out of contract activity and completed the work.) .If it's raining or bad weather, reschedule as the property is more susceptible to damage. .Do not let a truck or any other vehicle besides the skid steer on your lawn (unless you agree and do not mind the damage it may cause). .Make sure they only do what's contracted (ex. if cutting, not pull up a stump creating a hole). For all involved it's better to follow the contract, so make sure to ask detailed questions about exactly how the work will be carried out, and do not change anything in the plan agreed upon. I would not suggest handing over full payment until the job is completed. If it's in your contract to pay up front, ask to change it to half up front, half when done before approving the contract. If you're not fully agreed on the contract and payment, you may wish to look for other quotes. *Overall, the company will provide you with a completed project as requested. And Phyllis is an absolute gem, her customer service is fantastic!
