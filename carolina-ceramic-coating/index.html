What we do

The work behind the shine

Every job listed here is one Carolina Ceramic Coating | Columbia SC names on their own site. If yours is not on the list, the quickest answer is a phone call.

Ceramic coating

  • 3M ceramic coating on paint
  • Coating for glass, wheels and plastic trim
  • Coating over paint protection film and vinyl
  • Three year and five year coating options

Paint correction

  • Multi stage paint correction
  • Wet sanding
  • Swirl and scratch removal
  • Decontamination before coating

Exterior detailing

  • Exterior wash and wax
  • Bug and road tar removal
  • Clay bar treatment
  • Wheels and tyres cleaned and dressed

Interior detailing

  • Deep vacuum of seats and carpets
  • Shampoo for stains and odours
  • Leather cleaned and conditioned
  • Steam clean of dash, doors and cup holders

Window tinting

  • Full vehicle window tinting
  • Windshield tint
  • Ceramic window tint installation
  • Tint removal and replacement

Paint protection and finishing

  • Paint sealant for gloss and water beading
  • Auto waxing
  • Headlight restoration
  • Engine bay clean and degrease

Larger vehicles

  • RV detailing
  • Boat detailing
